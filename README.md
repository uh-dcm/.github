# Digital and Computational methods at the University of Helsinki

We host various [courses](https://github.com/topics/uh-dcm-teaching) and [tools](https://github.com/topics/uh-dcm-tools) for digital and computational social science.

This organisation is for public consumption and the materials here should be ready for use.
Our more research oriented materials, which are under more extensive development are in our [research group organisation](https://github.com/uh-soco).
